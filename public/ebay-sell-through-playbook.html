Bottom line up front
  1. Make the second card cheap to add. A combined shipping rule (first card pays shipping, each extra card adds a small amount or nothing) plus eBay Standard Envelope is what lets card sellers batch up to 15 raw cards into one envelope for about a dollar.
  2. Plan for the 3-ounce wall. eBay Standard Envelope stops at 3 oz, and eBay has no setting that switches a buyer to Ground Advantage automatically. Sellers cap the rule, raise the first-card price, or set price bands (≤$20 ships envelope, over $20 ships Ground Advantage).
  3. Reward bigger carts. Store subscribers use order-size discounts ("buy 3, save 15%"), volume pricing and free shipping over a spend threshold. eBay suggests setting thresholds just above your average order.
  4. Automate offers. Set Best Offer auto-accept and auto-decline from your costs, and send offers to watchers automatically.
  5. Rank in search: complete item specifics, keyword titles, competitive total cost (price plus shipping), free shipping on fixed-price listings, fast handling time, and clean seller metrics. Promoted Listings help, but attribution changes in 2026 made them more expensive.
  6. Keep inventory fresh. Sellers commonly end and "Sell Similar" listings older than about 90 days or 6 months after rechecking comps, though eBay has cautioned this may hurt more than it helps. List new cards every day or week.
Part 01

What "sell-through" means and how to measure it

Sell-through rate is items sold divided by items listed over a period: 30 sales against 100 active listings in a month is 30%.1 It's a velocity measure, and velocity multiplies your margin over time.1

eBay's Product Research tool in Seller Hub (formerly Terapeak) is free and calculates a sell-through rate for any search,2 but only for items sold in the last 90 days or less, and eBay doesn't publish its exact formula.3 eBay doesn't show a per-listing sell-through rate for your own store, so you build it from Seller Hub reports.4

30–50%Healthy 30-day sell-through target cited by one reseller guide5
<15%Same guide: a sign of a systemic pricing, category or listing problem5
90 daysProduct Research's sell-through window3

There's no official eBay benchmark, so treat these figures as rules of thumb.4 Low-dollar base cards will naturally run lower than hits, so track sell-through by tier (hits, mid-tier, lots).

Part 02

Shipping settings that batch multiple cards

Combined shipping lets you put several items in one package instead of several, which cuts postage and gives buyers a reason to add more to their cart.6 Trading cards are one of the categories where it works best, because buyers naturally want several.7

The building blocks

Foundation

eBay Standard Envelope (ESE)

Tracked envelope service for raw cards: $0.69 (1 oz), $0.97 (2 oz), $1.25 (3 oz). eBay's help page caps it at 15 raw cards and no more than 2 toploaders per envelope.8 It includes up to $20 of protection on single-item orders and $50 on combined orders.9

Note: eBay's envelope-kit page says its 3-pocket kit ships "up to 24 cards" with ESE,9 which conflicts with the 15-card limit on the help page. Follow the help page.

Foundation

Shipping policies (business policies)

Discount rules attach to shipping policies, so every listing on a given policy gets the same combined-shipping behavior.10 Card sellers keep separate policies for ESE cards, Ground Advantage cards and lots.

The three discount rule types

RuleHow it worksUse it for
Flat ruleFirst item pays full shipping; each additional item adds a fixed amount (for example $1.50), or nothing.11Raw singles of similar weight. The standard card-seller setup.
Calculated ruleeBay combines item weights and prices the real parcel.12 It only applies to listings with both calculated shipping and combined discounts set.13Mixed inventory: singles plus slabs, lots or boxes.7
Promotional ruleFree or discounted shipping when an order passes a spend or item threshold. It overrides your flat and calculated rules, and buyers see it highlighted on listings.10"Free shipping over $25"-style offers that push buyers to add cards.

eBay's help page also mentions a "Max. shipping cost" option when creating a flat rule (noted there for eBay Live listings), which stops a 10-card order from charging ten add-on fees.10 One guide recommends stopping the add-on charge after three or four items and quoting larger orders manually.7

The 3-ounce wall

A recurring complaint: a buyer orders four cards, pays about $2.30 in combined shipping, but the package is over 3 oz and has to go Ground Advantage at about $5. An eBay mentor confirmed there's no setting that switches the buyer to Ground Advantage after a set number of cards, and suggested raising the first card's shipping charge so that by the fourth card it approaches the Ground Advantage rate.14 Other fixes sellers use:

  • Price bands. Cards at $20 and under get free shipping baked into the price and ship ESE; cards over $20 charge shipping and ship Ground Advantage.15 One guide warns against using ESE on any sale over $20.16
  • Watch the thickness. Each envelope pocket can hold more than one card. When a stack exceeds ESE's ¼-inch limit, one seller just adds a non-machinable stamp.17
  • Cap the add-on. Set a max shipping cost on the flat rule and quote big orders by hand.7
  • Check multi-quantity defaults. One seller reports eBay quietly changed how shipping is computed for multi-quantity purchases in January 2025, and that he had to add a "No Discount" rule to stop eBay combining weights and undercharging.18
  • Lots still qualify. eBay's shipping team confirmed ESE is live for the Mixed Card Lots category.19

Auctions and Best Offers

Automatic rules apply to Buy It Now checkout. For auction wins and accepted offers, sellers combine shipping by sending one invoice with adjusted shipping,12 or by issuing a partial refund if the buyer already paid separately.20 eBay also added a way for auction buyers to see that a seller offers combined shipping discounts before they bid.21

Part 03

Deals that make buyers add more cards

eBay's own 2026 holiday guidance suggests that dealers with big inventories of lower- and mid-priced cards use volume-based offers that grow the order instead of discounting individual cards.22 These tools sit under Seller Hub, Marketing, Discounts, and most require a store subscription.23

Stacking rule. When a cart qualifies for volume pricing, a codeless coupon and an order discount at once, eBay applies only the single best one.29 Design one primary multi-card offer rather than three overlapping ones.

The deal and the shipping rule should work together. If "buy 4, save 15%" pushes orders past 3 oz, you're back at the Ground Advantage problem, so check the tester above with your typical order size.

Part 04

Converting watchers: offers and ads

Best Offer, automated

Buyers can send offers, and you accept, decline or counter. Set auto-accept and auto-decline thresholds so only the middle band reaches you.30 One seller recommends basing both numbers on your costs rather than a flat percentage off, since the only numbers that matter are what you paid and what eBay takes.31 Another sets auto-decline at any price that would leave 10% margin or less.32

  • A worked example from one guide: on an $80 listing with a $58 floor, decline under $55 and accept over $72, so strong offers close instantly even at 3 a.m. The trade-off is that some lowball buyers simply leave.33
  • eBay's current help page gives you 24 hours to respond, not the 48 many older guides say.31
  • Buyers on eBay's forums complain about sellers who turn on Best Offer but auto-decline anything more than a penny off. If you turn it on, leave real room.34

Offers to watchers

Seller Hub can send offers to buyers who've shown interest, such as watchers or cart abandoners,35 and you can set offers to send automatically.36 Watchers only receive one offer each, so make it count.37 Tracking watchers and allowing offers are among the basic tips a card-selling newsletter gives for lifting sales.38

Promoted Listings in 2026

General (cost per sale)

Formerly "Standard." You pay your ad rate, a percentage of the total sale, only on an attributed sale.39 In trading card singles, 31% of items use General ads, per eBay's own listing-form insights.40

Priority (cost per click)

Formerly "Advanced." Since January 2026, only Priority ads can take the first ad slot at the top of search results.41 eBay also changed General attribution and warned sellers that General spend may rise.42

Seller reaction is mixed. One UK forum post calls both strategies skewed toward eBay, because the ad rate charged is the one at time of sale and attribution can hit items you didn't mean to promote heavily.43 A practical approach from one guide: work out the highest ad rate your margin can absorb before testing.44 On $1–3 cards that ceiling is usually near zero, so card sellers tend to promote hits and mid-tier cards and leave base unpromoted.

Part 05

Ranking in eBay search

eBay says Best Match considers how closely a listing matches the search, how popular the item is, price, listing quality and completeness, terms like return policy and handling time, and the seller's track record.45

Item specifics

eBay's search uses structured data (category, specifics, identifiers), so detailed specifics improve both findability and ranking.46 Filters like team, player, grade and parallel only work if you fill them in.

Total cost

The algorithm compares price plus shipping against competitors, and free shipping gets a small additional boost.47 eBay says free shipping can help fixed-price listings in some categories, but auctions get no search advantage from it.48

Handling time

A shorter handling time means an earlier estimated delivery and preference in search, but eBay warns not to shorten it if you can't meet it.46

Titles

Up to 80 characters; use them for words buyers search and details that set your card apart.49 The format from Report 1: year, brand, player, card number, parallel, grade, plus team.

Card conditions

eBay now requires its trading-card condition values and condition descriptors, including when relisting or using Sell Similar.50

Seller performance

Defect rate, late shipments and unresolved cases all feed ranking.51 Ship on time; one late-shipment streak costs more visibility than any title tweak gains.

Store categories

Store categories are your store's aisles. You can create up to 300 categories in three levels and assign each listing to two of them. eBay suggests 7–10 top-level categories with short names under 30 characters, organized the way buyers shop.56

One card seller assigns each card two categories, by year and by team, and team collectors click straight to their team.57 Common card setups also include "Rookie Cards," "Graded" and "By Player" sections.58 Team categories pair naturally with sale events: mark down a whole team's category before a big game.

Part 07

Stale listings and listing volume

ActionWhat happensWho uses it
ReviseSame listing, same item number and watchersPrice fixes.59
RelistKeeps watchersOne high-volume seller swears by a mass end-and-relist of listings older than 30 days, reporting a two-day jump in impressions and sales.60
Sell SimilarBrand-new item number, treated as a new listingCommon rules: end at about 90 days,61 or end listings older than 6 months and recheck comps before relisting.62

The practice is debated. eBay told sellers at eBay Open that end-and-relist may hurt more than it helps, while some eBay employees and many sellers still say listings go stale after 90 days.63 Sellers on r/eBaySellerAdvice say eBay won't suspend you for it, but the reposted listing is treated as new, which cuts both ways.64 The common ground: recheck comps and fix price, title and photos before relisting, rather than relisting unchanged.65

Keep the funnel full

Sell-through improves when fresh inventory keeps arriving. Reseller workflow guides recommend listing daily or on weekly targets and tracking sell-through by category.62 For cards, the bottleneck is listing time, which is what bulk tools address:

  • eBay's AI bulk listing for trading cards, officially launched in 2024 after years of delays.66
  • Card Dealer Pro (from CollX): feed cards into a scanner, it identifies them with AI, proposes title and price, and publishes to eBay.6768
  • Kronocard / Kronozio: bulk scanning, AI documentation and eBay sync. Its free plan includes 50 eBay listings, and a paid plan starts at $8.6970
Part 08

A weekly sell-through routine

  1. Shipping policies: ESE policy for cards ≤$20 with a flat combined rule and a cap; Ground Advantage policy for cards over $20 and slabs; calculated policy for lots and boxes.
  2. One headline multi-card offer: an order-size discount or free shipping above a threshold set just over your average order.2326
  3. Offers on autopilot: cost-based auto-accept/decline thresholds on every Best Offer listing; automatic offers to watchers.
  4. New listings with full item specifics, condition descriptors and team in the title, assigned to team and year store categories.
  5. Sale event on one store category tied to the week's games or news.
  6. Stale sweep: pull the oldest listings, recheck comps, fix price and photos, then Revise or Sell Similar.
  7. Ad check: confirm Promoted Listings rates still fit each tier's margin; drop ads from low-dollar cards.
  8. Measure: sell-through by tier, average order size, and how often orders hit the 3-ounce wall.
